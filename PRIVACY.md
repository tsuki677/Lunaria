# Privacy Policy — Lunaria

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Lunaria is an independent browser customization. It does not run analytics, advertising trackers, or background telemetry.

## Settings stored locally

Lunaria stores the preferences you choose in the browser's extension storage on your own device. These settings can include:
- The background color and accent color for the new-tab page.
- Names and HTTPS URLs of shortcut tiles you add.

Lunaria does not send these settings to the project author or a Lunaria-operated server.

## Search

The Lunaria new-tab page does not include a text-entry search form and does not collect or transmit search terms. It provides a link that opens the Google homepage. If you choose to search after arriving on Google's website, your interaction and query are handled directly by Google and subject to Google's own privacy terms. Lunaria does not read that query.

## Shortcut tiles

When you click a shortcut tile, your browser opens the website shown by that tile. The destination site receives the request according to its own policies. Custom shortcut URLs are restricted to HTTPS.

## Contact and updates

Lunaria has no account system, analytics endpoint, or project-operated data-collection service. Changes to this policy will be documented in the public Lunaria repository.
